About Casa King

Casa King has been on the Costa del Sol for 18 years. We have a large range of high quality pre-loved furniture, and new goods too: sofas, dining sets, beds, cabinets, wardrobes, outdoor furniture, kitchen appliances, mirrors and plenty more.

Whether you live here in Andalusia or are furnishing your holiday home, you can find good quality pieces at excellent prices. Our stock changes daily: see it today, gone tomorrow. Our staff will do their very best to help you find the item you want.
