About me

Hello, I'm Dámaris

El Arte del Tacto: tantric massage in Málaga that starts with real listening. Because the massage begins before the touch.

Who I am

I didn't come to massage from a book

I came from experience: years of professional sport, a time of deep change in my life and the process of reconnecting with my own body.

I went through a time when I also needed to stop and didn't know how. That's why I understand what most people bring with them when they arrive.

Sport is still part of my life. It taught me how the body moves, how it responds, when it needs pressure and when it needs calm.

“I am not a garden flower meant to be decorative; I am the wild root that breaks through the asphalt.”

Dámaris

My path

From professional sport to conscious touch

  1. Sport

    Years of professional sport

    I learned to listen to the body from the inside. The therapist's body is part of the method too.

  2. Life change

    A time to stop

    Reconnecting with my own body. That is where my way of working comes from: first listen, then touch.

  3. Training

    Still learning

    Not to collect titles: the body has more layers than any course can cover. My references are Mario Alonso Puig and Pablo d'Ors.

  4. Málaga · Barcelona · Madrid

    El Arte del Tacto

    My space in Málaga and, every two months, bookings in Barcelona and Madrid.

What drives me

Purpose, vision and mission

Purpose

A space where the nervous system is finally allowed to release what it has been storing up.

Vision

To be the place you come back to regularly, caring for your body before it has to speak louder.

Mission

Listen before touching. You know exactly what you are coming for and what will not happen, in a safe space with clear boundaries.

“When the body has a safe space, the mind rests on its own.”

The space

Soft light, plants, candles and silence

It isn't a spa. It's a space where the body can slow down without having to justify it.
