The Welcome Ritual · Listen

Before touching, there is listening. We talk using Nonviolent Communication: what you bring, your questions and your boundaries.

  1. The Welcome Ritual, listen: before touching, there is listening. We talk using Nonviolent Communication to get to know each other and agree on your boundaries.
  2. The Welcome Ritual, breathe: we breathe together until your nervous system slows down. The body needs that transition before it can let go.
  3. Conscious Touch: full presence and active listening to the body. I respond to what it needs, moment by moment.
  4. Feel again: a loose jaw, lowered shoulders, deep breathing. When the body has a safe space, the mind rests on its own.
