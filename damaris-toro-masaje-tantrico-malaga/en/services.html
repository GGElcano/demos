What a session is like

The art of touch begins long before contact

  1. I prepare the room

    Before I receive you, I settle into presence. Lighting a candle is not just lighting a room: it opens a temple of presence.

  2. The Welcome Ritual

    The session doesn't start on the futon: it starts with a conversation, breathing and a guided relaxation. What you are looking for, what you need and where your boundaries are. The body needs that transition before it can let go.

  3. Touch

    Full presence and deep listening are the only language. Your nervous system doesn't listen to your affirmations. It listens to your breath.

  4. Coming back to you

    When the body feels safe, it starts to release what it has been holding in silence.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask me most

What is a traditional tantric massage?

It is a full-body massage rooted in the Tantra tradition. It is built on conscious touch, breath and presence. It has no goal to reach: it helps the body feel safe enough to let go of what it has been holding and come back to you.

How much does a session cost?

Solar awakening and Lunar awakening: €100 (1 hour). Heart opening: €60 (45 minutes). Couples or four hands: €200 (1 hour). To book, message me on WhatsApp at +34 672 07 60 34.

Is it a safe space?

Yes. Everything is held in a safe, professional space of complete respect, where your boundaries and your pace matter most.

I have never had a tantric massage. Can I come?

Of course. No experience needed and nothing to prove. Before we begin, we talk about what you are looking for and about your boundaries.

Where is it?

In Málaga, Spain. I send you the exact address when your appointment is confirmed.

Do you work outside Málaga?

Yes. Every two months I open bookings in Barcelona and Madrid. I announce the dates on the blog and on Instagram.

Is there nudity during the session?

Yes. As in traditional tantra, the massage is received without clothes, because conscious touch moves across the whole body. Everything happens with clear consent, boundaries we agree before we start and complete respect. It is not a sexual service.

How do I book?

On WhatsApp at +34 672 07 60 34 or with a direct message on Instagram (@superdama79). Sessions are always by appointment.
