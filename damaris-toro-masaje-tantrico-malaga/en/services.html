Frequently asked questions

What people ask me most

What is a traditional tantric massage?

It is a full-body massage rooted in the Tantra tradition. It is built on conscious touch, breath and presence. It has no goal to reach: it helps the body feel safe enough to let go of what it has been holding and come back to you.

How much does a session cost?

It depends on the session and on what you need, so I'll tell you the price myself. Message me on WhatsApp at +34 672 07 60 34.

Is it a safe space?

Yes. Everything is held in a safe, professional space of complete respect, where your boundaries and your pace matter most.

I have never had a tantric massage. Can I come?

Of course. No experience needed and nothing to prove. Before we begin, we talk about what you are looking for and about your boundaries.
